Uses the iPhone camera and on-device pose estimation to draw a live 33-point body skeleton. Keep your full body visible, stand about 6–10 feet away, and use good lighting. This first version tracks body pose, not individual finger joints or true 6DoF VR controllers.

Privacy and compatibility: Camera access requires permission and the app to be opened over HTTPS in Safari. Video is processed in the browser; this feature does not upload or save camera footage. Keep the page open while tracking. It is a motion-tracking preview, not a system-wide controller for Roblox, Fortnite, or other apps.

Steam Link streams PC-rendered video over Wi-Fi; it does not run Windows Steam games on the iPhone. For best Steam Link performance, connect the gaming PC to the router by Ethernet and connect the iPhone to 5 GHz Wi-Fi. Bluetooth can handle pairing and supported audio/control features. Smooth video requires a glasses display input and a compatible video path such as USB-C DisplayPort or a supported wireless display receiver.
